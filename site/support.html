Common questions

How do I pay for something I grabbed?
Once you and the seller agree on a price, they send a payment request in your chat. Tap Pay on it to pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay or a card.

How do I get paid as a seller?
Go to Settings → Get paid and set up payouts with Stripe. Then tap $ in a chat to request payment.

How do I report or block someone?
Open their profile, tap the menu, and choose Report or Block. You can report posts and listings from their menu too.

How do I cancel my subscription?
In your iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions (or Google Play → Payments & subscriptions).

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Account → Delete account.
