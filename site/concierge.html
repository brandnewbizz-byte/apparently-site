Event nights and getaways, booked for you.

Concert and game tickets, flights, hotels and rides, put together as one package with one all-in price.

How it works

  1. 1
    Grab a package in the Apparently app.The price you see is the whole price: tickets or travel plus our planning and booking fee. No fees added later.
  2. 2
    Pay with our secure Stripe link.Your card is only held, not charged, while we book.
  3. 3
    We book it for you.We buy your tickets or book your trip in your name, usually within 24 hours, and send everything to your email.
  4. 4
    Then the charge goes through.Only once your tickets or bookings are secured.

✅ Delivery Guarantee

Your tickets on time, or you pay nothing. If we can't get what you grabbed at the price you saw, we release the hold on your card and you're never charged. If a charge has already gone through, you get every cent back.

Good to know

What we need from you. The email your tickets should go to. For flights: each traveler's full name exactly as on their ID, and date of birth.

If an event is cancelled, you get a full refund, including our fee. If it's postponed, your tickets carry over to the new date; if you can't make it, refunds follow the event's own policy.

Flights and hotels follow the airline's and hotel's change and cancellation rules. We tell you those rules before we book.

Who we are. Apparently Concierge is run by the Apparently team (Doseofit LLC, Newark, NJ) with help from Claude, an AI assistant. A person approves every purchase. We're not affiliated with Ticketmaster, venues, artists, teams, airlines or hotels.
