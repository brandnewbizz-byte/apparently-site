Delete your Apparently account

You can delete your account and data in the app, or ask us to do it for you here.

Option 1: in the Apparently app (fastest)

Open the app, tap Profile → the ⚙️ Settings gear → Account → Delete account, then confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

Option 2: ask us by email (no app needed)

Email concierge@nationalcompliancehub.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Apparently account” and your username. We confirm it's you and delete your account within 7 days, then email you when it's done.

Before you delete

  • Cancel any subscription (Basic, Plus or Pro) in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a store subscription.
  • If you sell on Apparently, your Stripe account is separate. Close it from your Stripe dashboard if you want to.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, username, photo, bio) and sign-in details.
  • Your posts, stories, comments, messages, voice notes, bundles, skill deals, requests, plans and bookings.
  • Your feed preferences and notification settings.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Records of payments and orders that we must keep for tax, accounting and fraud prevention, for up to 7 years.
  • Reports about abuse involving your account, for up to 1 year, to keep the community safe.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Messages you sent may still appear in the other person's chat history as “Deleted user”.
