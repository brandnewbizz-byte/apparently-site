Apparently

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information the Apparently app and website (“Apparently”, “we”, “us”) collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email concierge@nationalcompliancehub.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, name, username, profile photo and bio.
  • What you post and send: posts, stories, photos, videos, comments, messages, voice notes, bundles, skill deals, requests, plans, and reports you make.
  • Orders and payments: what you grab or sell, order status, payment requests and amounts. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number. If you sell on Apparently, Stripe collects the identity and bank details it needs to pay you.
  • Subscriptions: your plan and its status from the App Store or Google Play (through RevenueCat). We don't receive your payment card details.
  • Your feed choices: what you grab, skip, save or hide, and the kinds of plans you pick, so we can show you things you'll like.
  • Device information: a push notification token if you allow notifications, and basic technical data such as app version and crash information.
  • Camera, microphone and photos: only when you choose to take or share a photo, video or voice note, or join a call or Spot room.

We don't collect your precise location, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run the app: your profile, posts, messages, orders, payments and notifications.
  • To personalize your Home feed.
  • To keep Apparently safe: reviewing reports, blocking abuse, preventing fraud.
  • To contact you about your account, orders and important changes.

We don't sell your personal information and we don't show third-party ads.

Who we share it with

  • Other members: your profile and public posts are visible to people on Apparently. Messages are visible to the people in the conversation. When you grab or sell something, the other person sees your name and the order details.
  • Service providers who run parts of the app for us: Supabase (hosting and database), Stripe (payments and payouts), RevenueCat, Apple and Google (subscriptions), and Expo (push notifications). They may only use your information to provide their service.
  • The law: if required by law, or to protect people's safety and the security of the app.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile any time in Settings → Edit profile.
  • Turn notifications on or off in Settings → Notifications.
  • Block people in their profile, and see blocked accounts in Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account, or request deletion on the web. This deletes your profile, posts, messages, listings and bookings. Records we must keep by law (for example payment records) are kept only as long as required.
  • You can also email us to see, correct or delete your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your information within 30 days, except what we must keep for legal, tax or fraud-prevention reasons.

Security

Your information is stored with access controls and sent over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

Children

Apparently isn't for children. You must be at least 18 to use the marketplace and payments, and at least 13 to use Apparently at all. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
