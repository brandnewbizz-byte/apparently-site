1. Information we collect

What we collect depends on how you use the Service. Some information is needed to use Apparently at all (for example, an email address to create an account); other information is only collected if you choose to use a feature.

Information you give us

  • Account and profile: your email address, password (stored only in scrambled, “hashed” form by our sign-in provider), name, username, profile photo, bio, and anything else you add, such as a city or phone number. You can choose to make your profile private.
  • Sign-up choices: your agreement to our Terms, and the answers you give during onboarding, such as your age group and the topics you're into.
  • Content you create and share: posts, stories, photos, videos, captions, comments, likes, reactions, ratings, voice notes, Bundles, Skill Deals, listings, requests, plans and Planner entries, Spot rooms you create, and anything else you post, upload or send.
  • Messages: the messages, photos, videos and voice notes you send and receive, and when they were sent, delivered and read.
  • Orders and marketplace activity: what you grab, book, buy or sell, order details and status, payment requests, amounts, service fees, ratings and order history.
  • Payout and identity details for sellers: if you get paid through Apparently, our payment processor Stripe collects the identity, tax and bank details it needs. We receive only limited information back, such as whether your account is verified and able to receive payouts.
  • Concierge requests: what you ask Apparently Concierge to help with, and our conversation with you.
  • Reports, appeals and support: reports you make about people or content, blocks, appeals, and anything you tell us when you contact us.
  • Security settings: whether you turned on 2-step sign-in, and the devices you chose to “remember” (with a name for each device).

Information created when you use the Service

  • Activity: the posts, stories, videos, profiles, Bundles and Skill Deals you view, like, save, skip, hide, share or comment on; who you follow and who follows you; the Spot rooms you join; how long you watch; and the searches you make in the app.
  • Feed preferences: a taste profile built from your onboarding answers and your activity, used to choose what you see in your feeds. It is saved with your account so it works on every device you use.
  • Story and post views: when you view someone's story, they can see that you viewed it, as in other social apps.
  • Calls and live rooms: who called whom, when, whether a call was answered, how long it lasted, and who joined a Spot room. We do not record the sound or video of calls or rooms. See Section 6.
  • Subscriptions: your Apparently plan, its status and renewal date, received from Apple, Google and RevenueCat.

Device and technical information

  • Device details: device type, operating system and version, app version, language, time zone, and identifiers we create for your app install.
  • Notification tokens: if you allow notifications, a push token from Apple or Google (and, for calls on iPhone, a VoIP push token) so we can send you notifications and ring you for calls.
  • Network and log information: IP address, connection type, the date and time of requests, and the pages or features used, kept in our servers' logs. We may use an IP address to work out an approximate region, for example to fight fraud, but not your precise location.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports, error messages and performance information about the app.
  • Device permissions: with your permission, we access your camera, microphone and photos only when you use a feature that needs them (taking or sharing a photo, video or voice note, joining a call or a Spot room). We only access the photos and videos you pick. You can change these permissions in your phone's settings at any time.

We don't request your device's precise location (GPS), contacts, calendar, or advertising identifier, and we don't track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Information from others

  • Other members: people may mention or tag you, message you, send you payment requests, rate a deal with you, share content that includes you, or report your account or content.
  • Partners and service providers: Stripe (payment and payout status), and Apple, Google and RevenueCat (purchases and subscriptions).

To improve the Service

  • Understand how features are used, fix bugs and crashes, test new features, and measure performance.

To communicate with you

  • Send messages about your account, security codes, orders, payments, subscriptions, changes to our Terms or this policy, and replies to your requests.
  • With your permission, tell you about new features. You can turn these off at any time.

For legal reasons

  • Follow the law, respond to valid legal requests, enforce our Terms, keep tax and payment records, and protect the rights, property and safety of Apparently, our members and others.

3. How we use AI

Some features use artificial intelligence provided by OpenAI:

  • Post topics: when you share a public post, its caption and picture (or a video's cover image) may be analyzed to choose topics, spot content that may be sensitive, and help decide which posts to feature in feeds.
  • Planner ideas: when you ask for ideas, we send what's needed to make them, such as the city and day you're planning for, the kind of outing you picked, your free time, your general interests and your age group. We don't send your name, email or username.

OpenAI processes this information for us as a service provider. Under its API terms, it may not use information sent through its API to train its models. AI results can be wrong, so suggestions are a starting point, and people review reports and moderation decisions where it matters.

4. How information is shared

With other people on Apparently

  • Public information: your name, username, profile photo and bio can be seen by anyone on Apparently, even if your account is private. If your account is public, your posts, stories, Bundles, Skill Deals and the people you follow can be seen by anyone using the Service. Public content can be viewed, shared, saved or screenshotted by others, and may stay visible in other people's copies after you delete it.
  • Private accounts: if your account is private, only followers you approve can see your posts and stories and message you.
  • Messages and calls: are seen by the people in the conversation. Group chat and Spot room members can see who else is there.
  • Spot rooms: people in a room can see and hear what you say and show while your mic or camera is on, and see who is in the room.
  • Marketplace: when you buy or sell, the other person sees your name, profile and the order details they need, such as the time, place and notes for the service.
  • Ratings and comments: ratings, reviews, likes and comments you leave may be seen by others.

With service providers

We share information with companies that help us run the Service, listed in Section 5. They may use it only to provide their service to us, and must protect it.

For legal reasons and to prevent harm

We may access, keep and share information when we believe in good faith it's needed to: follow the law, a court order, subpoena or other legal process; respond to valid requests from law enforcement or government agencies; detect, stop and deal with fraud, abuse, security issues and violations of our Terms; or protect the rights, property and safety of Apparently, our members or anyone else, including to prevent death or serious harm.

Business changes

If Doseofit LLC is involved in a merger, sale, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy or sale of some or all of its assets, information may be transferred as part of that deal. This policy will keep applying to it unless you're told otherwise.

With your consent

We share information in other ways when you tell us to, for example when you share content outside Apparently.

We don't sell your personal information, we don't share it with third parties for their own marketing, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Calls, live rooms and Spot

  • Voice and video calls and Spot room voice and video are sent directly between devices where possible, encrypted in transit. When a direct connection isn't possible, they pass through a relay server that can't read the encrypted sound or video.
  • We do not record or store the sound or video of calls or Spot rooms. We keep call details (who, when, how long) and room membership, as described in Section 1.
  • Other people could record a call or room with another device or app without our knowledge. Only share what you're comfortable with.
  • Spot TV: videos played in a Spot room come from YouTube. When a video plays, YouTube receives information such as your IP address and device details under Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Payments and the marketplace

  • Card payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe uses your information under the Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • Subscriptions bought in the app are handled by Apple or Google. We get your plan and its status, not your payment details.
  • We keep records of orders, payments, refunds and payouts as required for accounting, tax, fraud prevention and dispute handling, even after your account is deleted.

8. Your choices and controls

  • Profile: edit your profile any time in Settings → Edit profile.
  • Private account: in Settings → Account privacy, make your account private so only followers you approve see your posts and stories and can message you, approve or decline follow requests, and hide view counts.
  • Blocking and muting: block people from their profile; see and manage blocked accounts in Settings. Mute people in a Spot room just for you.
  • Your content: delete posts, stories, comments and messages you sent. Deleted content is removed from the Service, but copies may remain for a short time in backups, or with people who already saved it.
  • Feed: hide posts and tell us you're not interested; your taste profile changes as you use the app.
  • Notifications: choose which notifications you get in Settings → Notifications, or turn them off in your phone's settings.
  • Device permissions: turn camera, microphone and photos access on or off in your phone's settings.
  • 2-step sign-in: turn it on, and remove remembered devices, in Settings.
  • Emails: if we send you emails about new features, each will have an unsubscribe link. You'll still get account, security and order emails.

12. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as we need it to provide the Service and for the purposes in this policy. For example:

  • Account and content: while your account is open. Content you delete is removed from the Service right away and from backups within about 30 days.
  • After you delete your account: we delete your information within 30 days, except what's described below.
  • Payment, order and tax records: as long as tax, accounting and payment laws require (often up to 7 years).
  • Safety: information about accounts removed for breaking our Terms may be kept longer to stop them coming back and to protect others.
  • Legal holds: information needed for a legal claim, investigation or request is kept until it's resolved.
  • Logs and diagnostics: server logs and crash reports are kept for a limited time and then deleted.

13. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app in Settings → Account → Delete account, or request deletion on the web. This permanently deletes your profile, posts, stories, messages you sent, listings, plans and bookings. It can't be undone. Records we must keep by law are kept as explained in Section 12. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account. If you have a subscription, cancel it in the App Store or Google Play as well.

14. Security

We protect your information with measures such as encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), access controls that limit who can read each kind of data, hashed passwords, optional 2-step sign-in, and limits on sign-in attempts. Messages are stored on our servers so they can be delivered across your devices and are not end-to-end encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password, turn on 2-step sign-in, and tell us right away at concierge@nationalcompliancehub.com if you think your account has been accessed without your permission. If a data breach affects you, we'll notify you as the law requires.

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete the account and its information. If you believe a child under 13 is using Apparently, please email us. Members aged 13 to 17 should use Apparently with a parent's or guardian's involvement.
